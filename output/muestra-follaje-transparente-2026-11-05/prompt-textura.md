# Textura de follaje recortado

Generada con la herramienta integrada de imágenes, con transparencia real. A partir de esa imagen se prepararon 24 cuadros con ondulación local para el archivo GIF de textura; no es una animación generada directamente por el modelo de imágenes.

Create one square seamlessly tiling game foliage CUTOUT texture on a genuinely transparent alpha background. NOT a tree scene. Full-frame scattered interlocking small deciduous leaves with a few fine branching twigs, creating an irregular airy foliage lace with 40 percent of the image fully transparent gaps DISTRIBUTED throughout the entire tile. Pale neutral silver-gray to off-white leaves, soft visible veins, painted stylized realistic game art, no green because the game tints them per tree. Leaves are 3-7 percent of image width, some small overlapping clusters, roughly 60 percent coverage, NO single central branch arrangement, no frame or circular silhouette. Leaves and twigs solid opaque; between them truly empty transparent holes, including at all four tile edges. Flat diffuse albedo, no cast shadow, no background color, no checkerboard drawn in image, no text, no outlines. It will be applied to layered low-poly tree canopies so background and other foliage can be seen through the holes. Tileable density across image.
