## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished 2-column by 3-row presentation contact sheet of a proposed MATERIAL RETEXTURING for an existing low-poly first-person escape game. The input image is the ACTUAL SIX ROOM reference contact sheet, same order. Preserve the room geometry, simple low-poly silhouettes, camera compositions, props, game symbols and general layout as closely as possible. This is a feasible surface-material paintover, not new architecture or a photorealistic redesign. Keep the stylized game rendering, but add restrained tactile detail and separate matte surfaces from selectively reflective metal. No new furniture, windows or structures. Large readable panel labels: FEARLESS, 1989, REPUTATION, LOVER, FOLKLORE / EVERMORE, MIDNIGHTS. Top small caption in Spanish: PROPUESTA CONCEPTUAL · NO IMPLEMENTADA. Dark charcoal thin gutters, attractive editorial layout, each panel equally sized.
Panel 1 Fearless: ivory fine-fiber wallpaper replacing yellow flat wall, lower honey oak wood paneling respecting existing wall division, matte warm oak floorboards with real fine grain, dark honey wood pedestals with brushed brass accents and same dark symbol discs, preserve hanging string lights, butterflies, picture frames and book. Warm inviting golden light.
Panel 2 1989: preserve same city illustrated window panels, cyan and pink neon strips, bus stop and bench. Fine blue-gray concrete and satin enamel walls, charcoal fine-grained terrazzo floor instead of synthetic glossy black, brushed steel street furniture. Preserve bright neon, recover surface detail in highlights. Urban dusk.
Panel 3 Reputation: same throne, serpents, wall poster and letter mirror tiles. Keep wall fan and diamond geometry but recolor wall to charcoal with restrained aged-gold art-deco lines, pewter vertical strips instead of green. Satin black stone floor with sparse veins, burgundy velvet throne and drapes, dark silver scaly snakes. Keep letter tiles high-contrast. Dramatic dark luxury, clearly distinct from golden Fearless.
Panel 4 Lover: preserve pink-and-cream checker floor, pastel rainbow, heart decorations, plinths and cloud lamps. Dusty rose and cream satin ceramic checker tiles with fine grout; matte powder pink wallpaper with extremely subtle watercolor clouds, pastel lacquer plinths with small pearlescent details. Reduced white bloom haze so shapes remain visible. Gentle pastel light, no radical lighting redesign.
Panel 5 Folklore/Evermore: retain SAME simple conical and faceted tree silhouettes and tree placements, no new realistic leaf geometry. Add muted vertical bark detail to plain trunks, leaf-litter and compacted earth to ground, mossy forest greens, glimpses of snow to right as in actual reference. Warm lantern atmosphere and cool dark forest backdrop. Preserve sign on central trunk.
Panel 6 Midnights: preserve giant clock markings and hands on floor, simple chairs and circular rug at left, hanging disco ball, wall clocks and star ceiling. Ink-blue fine stone floor, subtle midnight jacquard walls, walnut chair frames, woven blue upholstery, plum velvet and aged-brass clocks. Silver disco ball. Retain dark midnight atmosphere but material details readable.
Avoid photorealism, added ornate architecture, cinematic depth blur, unreadable letter game tiles, new objects, enlarged rooms, extra panels.
